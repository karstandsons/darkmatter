# The Darkmatter and axiond names

The code is open source (MIT or Apache-2.0, your choice). The names are not:
"Darkmatter", "axiond" and the Darkmatter icon identify the official project
by Karst & Sons. Neither license grants rights to them (Apache-2.0 says so in
section 6).

**Fine without asking**
- Saying a fork, plugin or package is "based on Darkmatter" or "compatible with axiond".
- Packaging the official, unmodified releases for a distribution under the same name.
- Talking about the project, linking to it, writing reviews and tutorials.

**Please rename**
- A fork or modified build you distribute: give it a name of its own, with its
  own icon, so people don't mistake it for the official app.

**Ask first**
- Anything else that could look official: app stores, hosted services,
  merchandise. Open an issue or email cody@karstandsons.com.
